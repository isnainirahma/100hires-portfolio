# Justin Welsh

## Why I Included Him

Justin Welsh consistently shares practical insights on audience growth, personal branding, and content systems. His content is highly relevant to this project because many B2B SaaS founders use LinkedIn as a primary channel for building authority and generating demand.

## Initial Observations

After reviewing several recent posts, a few recurring themes stood out:

- Consistency matters more than viral reach.
- Personal experience tends to outperform generic advice.
- Most posts use short, easy-to-scan formatting.
- Educational content is prioritized over direct promotion.

## Research Focus

While analyzing Justin Welsh's content, I want to better understand:

1. How high-performing posts are structured.
2. What hook formats appear most frequently.
3. How storytelling is combined with educational content.
4. Which content frameworks could be adapted for B2B SaaS companies.
