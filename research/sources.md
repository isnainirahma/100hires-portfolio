# Research Sources

## Project Topic

LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

## Expert Selection Criteria

Experts were selected based on the following criteria:

* Active and consistent LinkedIn publishing
* Strong experience in B2B SaaS, marketing, growth, or demand generation
* Practical insights rather than purely theoretical content
* Recognized influence within the B2B marketing community
* Content relevant to audience growth, brand building, and pipeline generation

## Research Scope

For each expert, the following content types will be reviewed:

* Recent LinkedIn posts
* Content structure and formatting
* Engagement and hook patterns
* Storytelling techniques
* Educational frameworks
* Demand generation and audience-building strategies

The objective is to identify recurring patterns that can be adapted into a practical LinkedIn organic growth playbook for B2B SaaS companies.
