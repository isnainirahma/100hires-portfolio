# LinkedIn Organic Content Research for B2B SaaS

## Overview

This repository contains structured research on LinkedIn organic content strategies within the B2B SaaS space.

The goal of this project is to analyze how top creators build audience, generate engagement, and create demand through consistent content.

---

## Research Structure

The repository is organized as follows:
research/
├── sources.md
├── insights.md
├── linkedin-posts/
├── youtube-transcripts/

---

## What This Project Includes

- 10 LinkedIn creators in B2B SaaS and marketing
- Analysis of selected LinkedIn posts from each expert
- Supporting YouTube content summaries and key takeaways
- Cross-expert synthesis of content patterns

---

## Key Insights

From the analysis, several consistent patterns were identified:

- Consistency is more important than virality
- Strong hooks significantly impact engagement
- Educational content performs better than promotional content
- Personal experience increases trust and relatability
- Simple and clear messaging improves performance

---

## Objective

The objective of this project is to identify repeatable patterns in LinkedIn content strategy that can be used as a foundation for building a practical framework for B2B SaaS growth.

---

## Notes

All insights are based on publicly available content and qualitative analysis of content patterns across multiple creators.
